Welcome to Mīrāth

Inheritance, made clearer.

Mīrāth is designed to help you understand and calculate Islamic inheritance shares through a clear and structured experience.

How Mīrāth Works

Three simple steps from estate to result.

  1. 1
    Enter estateProvide the estate value, debts, funeral expenses, and any will or bequest information.
  2. 2
    Add heirsSelect the relevant heirs and enter their counts and details.
  3. 3
    Review resultsSee each heir’s share, fractions, explanations, and any warnings or review notes.

Only supported scenarios are calculated automatically. Complex or unsupported situations may show a review warning.

Before You Begin

Mīrāth is a calculation assistance tool. It is not a replacement for a qualified Islamic scholar, a religious authority, or a legal professional.

Results depend on the information you enter, the selected calculation policy or method, and the scenarios the application supports. Some inheritance cases may require further scholarly review, so please verify important matters before acting on any result.

  1. 1
    Accurate informationProviding accurate information is your responsibility.
  2. 2
